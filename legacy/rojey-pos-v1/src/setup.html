Rojey POS setup

Type your POS website and choose your printers. You can come back here any time from the menu (Ctrl + ,).

1. Your POS website

The address you open in the browser, for example pos.rojeycreamery.com. If you move the POS to a new domain, change it here.

2. Printers

Receipts go to the receipt printer. Kitchen tickets (KOT) go to the kitchen printer. Leave a printer on "Ask every time" to get the normal print window.

Type the kitchen name exactly as it shows on the ticket (for example Bar), then pick its printer.
